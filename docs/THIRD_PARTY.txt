Android TV Remote Service v2 implementation:
androidtvremote2 0.3.2 (Apache-2.0), https://github.com/tronikos/androidtvremote2
Sofa uses an adapter around this library; its TLS context is replaced with a
context that verifies the TV certificate approved in Sofa. Dependencies are
pinned with hashes in Resources/requirements.lock. Included license texts:
aiofiles (Apache-2.0, including NOTICE), cryptography (Apache-2.0 OR BSD-3-Clause),
cffi (MIT-0), pycparser (BSD-3-Clause), protobuf (BSD-3-Clause).
