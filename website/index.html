Different TVs.
The same comfy seat.

Support follows the TV’s operating system. Here’s where the beta stands.

Hisense VIDAA

Remote, app and input catalogs, and direct volume control. Tested on one Hisense A4K-series TV; other models and firmware need testing.

Device tested

Google TV & Android TV

Any brand with Android TV Remote Service v2. Pairing, navigation, playback, and available volume feedback. No ADB or developer mode. Physical-device testing is still needed.

Experimental

Samsung Tizen

New in build 13. Secure network remote control with on-TV Allow approval. Glass volume slider when compatible feedback is available; −/+ otherwise. App lists vary by model. No physical Samsung TV testing yet; older Orsay models and network wake aren’t supported.

Experimental

Roku, Fire TV & Xumo

These platforms aren’t supported yet. A Hisense badge alone doesn’t establish compatibility.

From download to downtime

Make yourself
comfortable.

  1. 01

    Move in.

    Open the DMG, drag Sofa to Applications, then eject the disk image and open Sofa. Find Sofa in your menu bar.

  2. 02

    Find your TV.

    Use the same home network and allow Local Network access. Let Sofa discover your TV or enter its local IP address.

  3. 03

    Pair. Play. Relax.

    Follow the prompts: enter the TV’s code for VIDAA or Android TV, or choose Allow on Samsung. Give it a name and you’re ready to go.

A little help getting comfortable ↗

0.5.0-beta.1 · Build 13

An early seat at the table.

This beta is Developer ID signed by TOP NOTCH COMPANY LIMITED. Build 13 is Apple notarized. The download contains the approved app with its notarization ticket stapled, verified with Gatekeeper. Drag Sofa to Applications and open it normally; macOS may ask you to confirm opening a downloaded app.

Compatibility varies by TV and app. YouTube seeking is verified on the test VIDAA TV; other apps can use saved media-button or Left/Right control modes. Google / Android TV and Samsung Tizen support have automated protocol checks but no physical-device validation yet. Samsung’s slider requires a compatible volume service; native volume buttons remain available when it is missing.
